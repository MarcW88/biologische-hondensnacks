## Reader job
Een puppy-eigenaar moet treats kunnen kiezen voor training/kauwen terwijl de complete groeivoeding dominant blijft en fysieke veiligheidsrisico's worden vermeden.

## Evidence
- Merck Feeding Practices: puppy's hebben complete/balanced growth diet nodig tot skeletal maturity; small/medium vaak 8–12m, large/giant 10–16m; overfeeding growth undesirable: https://www.merckvetmanual.com/management-and-nutrition/nutrition-small-animals/feeding-practices-in-small-animals
- Merck Puppy Care: groei vraagt puppyvoeding met passende energie/nutriënten: https://www.merckvetmanual.com/dog-owners/routine-care-of-dogs/puppy-care
- FEDIAF treats/chews complementair, niet complete; size/shape and supervision: https://europeanpetfood.org/pet-food-facts/fact-sheets/quality-and-safety/chews-and-treats-for-dogs/
- WSAVA treat safety: bones, excessively hard chews, raw untreated animal treats risks: https://wsava.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Feeding-treats-to-your-dog-v2.pdf
