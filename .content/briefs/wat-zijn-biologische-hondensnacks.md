## Reader and outcome
Dutch dog owner new to organic claims. Explain production first, distinguish
product organic status from individual organic ingredients, then separate that
from nutritional suitability. No product ranking, fictitious test or medical advice.

## Value to preserve
Agricultural-weight denominator, other production conditions, qualified logo
rule, distinction from natural, complementary feeding role, existing useful links,
URL/title/canonical, noindex and existing visual components.

## Evidence before writing
Commission organic production page: farming, livestock welfare/health rules,
authorised inputs, separation during processing. Consulted 22 October 2026.
USDA EU report E42023-0049: threshold, ingredient-list-only references below it,
prepacked logo and 1 May 2024. Defra memorandum PE-CONS 33/23 REV 1 confirms
threshold and date. These are explanatory government documents about the EU
regulation, not UK rules substituted for Dutch rules. EUR-Lex direct access
remains protected; no claim of exhaustive legal certification.
NVWA: natural is a product descriptor. FEDIAF: treats are typically complementary.

## Scope derived from evidence
Simplify opening; replace repetitive answer box with a usable reading rule.
Explain plant cultivation, animal-source ingredients and processing in plain
prose. Shorten legal background; add explicitly fictional arithmetic for the
agricultural denominator, with a warning that the threshold alone is insufficient.
Keep the main architecture and practical links. Add contextual ingredient-reading
link and precise sources near the relevant claims. No word or section quota.

## Boundaries and risks
Definition/production here; label checking and natural-vs-organic comparison remain
on their own pages. Do not claim zero pesticide residue, no veterinary treatment,
no additives, local origin or better health for every dog. Do not invent a product
label. Do not describe the dietary rules for farm animals as rules for the dog.

## Acceptance
Reader can explain production with concrete mechanisms, understand the denominator,
recognise that the percentage is one condition, and find the appropriate next guide.
Corrections survive npm run build without replacing authored content. Run separate
post-writing factual, linking, Humanizer, general-writing, anti-slop, SEO and final
Guide analysis publication-review passes. No indexation authorisation.
